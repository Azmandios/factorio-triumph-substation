# Triumph Substations (2.1 port)

Three advanced substations for Factorio 2.1: bigger supply areas, longer wire
reach, more expensive recipes — and one of them goes off like an atomic bomb.

This is a community update of **Triump Substations** by **Logvinov Alecksey**
(Factorio 1.1). The original idea, sprites and balance are his; this mod only
ports it to 2.1 and keeps it working.

## What it adds

**Advanced Substation** — 32×32 supply area, wire reach 32, 200 HP.
A solid upgrade over the vanilla substation.

**Experimental Nucleonic Substation** — 48×48 supply area, wire reach 48, 100 HP.
Works with roboports. **Explodes with a full atomic blast when destroyed.**

**Reckless Substation** — 64×64 supply area, wire reach 64, 100 HP.
The biggest coverage in the mod. Use at your own risk.

## Research

- **Advanced Substations** (`electric-substation-3`) — requires Electric Energy
  Distribution 2 and Advanced circuits.
- **Nucleonic Substations** (`electric-substation-4`) — requires Advanced
  Substations and Nuclear power.
- **Reckless Substations** (`electric-substation-5`) — requires Nucleonic
  Substations and Nuclear power.

## Compatibility

- Factorio **2.1** (developed and tested on 2.1.24).
- No dependencies. Space Age is optional.
- Internal prototype names are unchanged (`substation-2/3/4`,
  `electric-substation-3/4/5`), so saves and blueprints from the original 1.1
  mod keep working.
- The Mod Portal name is `TriumphSubstation2` because `TriumphSubstation` is
  already taken by the 1.1 version.

## Installation

Subscribe in game (Mods → Install mods), or put the zip into your mods folder
and enable **Triumph Substations (2.1 port)**.
